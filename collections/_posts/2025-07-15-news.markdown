Prasita and Minghai's [paper](https://prasitagit.github.io/papers/SynverPreprint.pdf) on SynVer, which uses LLMs to generate C programs together with machine-checked proofs of their correctness, has been accepted to [ASE '25](https://conf.researchr.org/home/ase-2025). Congratulations!
