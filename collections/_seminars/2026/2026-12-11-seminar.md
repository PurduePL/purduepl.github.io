Last December I posted a short video every day about a different compiler optimization. Some were obvious, some surprised me, a handful caught me out completely, and some were compiler bugs. This talk distils the most interesting into a story about where modern optimizers are scary good, where they're stubbornly bad, and where that gap has moved. We'll investigate a number of interesting cases interactively with Compiler Explorer, and I'll go into some details on how the series was filmed and edited too.
